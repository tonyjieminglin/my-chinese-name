About My Chinese Name

Language deserves
to be treated with care.

We believe a Chinese name should be understandable to the person who carries it—and respectful of the language it comes from.

Our point of view

Personal meaning.
Linguistic care.

My Chinese Name is a personalised naming service for people who want a thoughtful connection with Chinese language and culture. We look at sound, character meaning, tone and cultural context to develop names that are more than literal translations.

We aim to explain our choices in clear, accessible English. We also recognise that Chinese is diverse: naming preferences and associations can vary across regions, generations and families. Where there is no single answer, we will be transparent about that.

Our commitments

  • Explain the meaning and pronunciation of every proposed name.
  • Avoid awkward character combinations and misleading claims.
  • Respect personal preferences without relying on stereotypes.
  • Be clear about what the service does—and does not—provide.

A note on expertise

Before public launch, the service operator should identify the actual people responsible for reviewing names and accurately describe their language experience. We do not publish invented credentials, native-speaker claims or cultural endorsements. Each name should be checked by a suitably competent Chinese-language reviewer before delivery.

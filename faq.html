Questions, answered

A few things worth
knowing first.

If you are new to Chinese names, these answers will help you understand the process and what to expect.

Do you simply translate my English name?

No. English and Chinese sounds do not map neatly onto one another. We consider pronunciation, character meanings and whether the complete name feels natural in Chinese. Sometimes a sound-based adaptation is useful; sometimes a meaning-led approach works better.

Do I need to know Chinese?

No. Your name explanation is written in English and includes Chinese characters, Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks and pronunciation guidance.

Will the name be suitable for official documents?

Our service provides a cultural name suggestion, not legal identity advice. Rules for official records vary by country and institution. Do not replace your legal name on passports, immigration records or other official documents based on our suggestion.

Can I request a particular meaning or style?

Yes. You can describe qualities, themes or styles that matter to you. We will explain if a preference creates a linguistic or cultural trade-off.

How long does it take?

We will confirm an estimated delivery time when responding to your enquiry. Turnaround depends on the package and the complexity of the brief.

Is payment taken when I submit the form?

No. The current website collects an enquiry only. We will confirm the scope and payment instructions separately. No payment is taken through this site at launch.

Can I ask for a revision?

The Personalised and Signature packages include one reasonable revision. The exact scope will be confirmed before payment.

Do you use birth dates or fortune-telling systems?

No birth date is required. We do not claim that names predict destiny, change fortune or guarantee outcomes.
